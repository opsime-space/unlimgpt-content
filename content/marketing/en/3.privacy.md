Do not include passwords, API keys, payment credentials, excessive personal data or information that cannot lawfully be processed for your intended use. Medical, financial, biometric and other sensitive data require checking applicable requirements and agreeing on necessary terms first. You must have a legal basis and provide necessary notices for other people's data you submit.

## 6. Saved history and API requests

Web chat saves conversations and results so you can continue working. Responses API response storage is enabled by default. `store: false` limits response storage for later retrieval by identifier but does not mean no processing or no records: usage information is retained, and a conversation-bound request may be saved in its history.

With `store: false` and an `Idempotency-Key`, a result may be temporarily retained to support a safe retry without a second paid generation, for no longer than 24 hours from initial acceptance. This is a specific retry window, not the retention period for all data. API use does not override external processing systems' rules.

Some automatic retries also retain an encrypted result record with a short two-minute retry window, extended on an actual retry. When that window ends, the record becomes unavailable for retry but may remain archived; no separate retention period for that archive is currently established. The end of the retry window does not mean complete erasure of the result.

Message content, responses, passwords and API keys are excluded from intended technical logging and tracing; diagnostics use limited Service operation information. Saving a conversation in the product is different from logging: no text in a log does not mean no text in saved history.

## 7. Retention and archiving

Saved history remains in the Service while its storage applies. **There is currently no automatic erasure period for saved content.** Account, accounting, security and communication records may be needed after access ends for legal obligations and disputes. This draft does not specify a retention period for those categories or backups.

Archiving a conversation, message or saved response hides it from ordinary access but retains its record and content. **Archiving is not complete erasure.** Self-service erasure of an account and all related data is not currently provided in the interface. Revoking a session or API key ends access but does not erase history either.

Backups and mandatory accounting records are considered separately; archiving does not rewrite existing copies. When handling a data request, we must explain what was done, what continues to be retained and on what basis, and which requirements remain unfulfilled. The technical absence of an erasure operation does not remove statutory rights.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and similar mechanisms support sign-in and security, language, theme and interface preferences. Local storage may retain reasoning level, tool choices, search depth, search domains and other preferences. A guest request draft, together with its selected model and settings, may temporarily remain in your tab's `sessionStorage` for transfer after registration; it is not sent for generation before an authorized action.

When reCAPTCHA runs, third-party protection may process technical data and use its own storage mechanisms. Its use is associated with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms). Third-party mechanisms are not all necessarily essential just because they relate to security: any legally required consent must be addressed separately.

Browser settings let you restrict or remove cookies and local data. This may sign you out, reset preferences or prevent protected operations. Temporary drafts and local preferences are not public disclosures, but may be accessible to people with access to your device. This policy does not replace separate consent for optional cookies where required.
